Natural & Home Solutions (Desi Ilaaj)

Zero-chemical organic remedies you can make at home | घर पर बनने वाले प्राकृतिक उपाय

Zero-Chemical
🧄 Garlic & Chilli Spray
Problem: Sucking insects & aphids.
Recipe: Grind 100g garlic + 5 hot chillies in 1L water with 5ml soap.
How to use: Spray every 5 days on leaf undersides.
Suitable: Tomato, Chilli, Wheat
🌿 Neem Oil Solution
Problem: Caterpillars & whiteflies.
Recipe: Mix 5ml neem oil + 2ml liquid soap in 1L water.
How to use: Spray in the evening after 4 PM.
Universal for All Crops
🪵 Wood Ash Barrier
Problem: Snails, caterpillars, cutworms.
Recipe: Clean dry wood ash from chulha.
How to use: Sprinkle a 2-inch ring around plant stems.
Vegetable Beds & Nurseries
🍊 Citrus Peel Spray
Problem: Ants & soft aphids.
Recipe: Soak orange/lemon peels in warm water overnight.
How to use: Strain and spray directly on affected leaves.
All Vegetable Crops
🪴 Tobacco Leaf Water
Problem: Sucking insects.
Recipe: Boil 50g tobacco leaves in 2L water for 20 mins.
How to use: Dilute 1:10 with water before spraying.
Cotton & Vegetables
🫚 Mustard Oil Emulsion
Problem: Scale bugs and mites.
Recipe: 2 spoons soap + 1 cup mustard oil in 1L water.
How to use: Shake well and spray on stems.
Fruit Trees & Pulses
🥛 Raw Milk Spray
Problem: Powdery white mold on leaves.
Recipe: 1 cup raw milk in 9 cups water.
How to use: Spray in bright morning sunlight once a week.
Squash, Cucumber, Tomato
🧂 Baking Soda Spray
Problem: Leaf spots & black rot.
Recipe: 1 spoon baking soda + 3 drops soap in 1L water.
How to use: Changes leaf pH so fungus cannot grow.
Tomato & Rose
🍵 Turmeric-Lime Paste
Problem: Stem rot and wound infection.
Recipe: Turmeric powder + slaked lime (choona) + water paste.
How to use: Paint onto cut branches and wounds.
Apple & Fruit Orchards
🍶 Fermented Buttermilk (Chhachh)
Problem: Rust and leaf blight.
Recipe: Keep sour buttermilk in a copper vessel for 4 days.
How to use: Dilute 1:5 in water and spray on crop.
Wheat, Mustard, Paddy
🌾 Fermented Cow Urine (Gomutra)
Problem: Fungal spore spread.
Recipe: Mix 1 part cow urine in 10 parts clean water.
How to use: Traditional antifungal barrier spray.
Traditional Natural Farming
🌿 Aloe Vera Gel Coat
Problem: Seedling damping-off.
Recipe: Blend fresh aloe pulp in water.
How to use: Dip young roots before transplanting.
Nursery Seedlings
🐛 Vermicompost Liquid Tea
Problem: Low soil life and poor roots.
Recipe: Soak 2kg vermicompost in 10L water for 24 hours.
How to use: Strain and pour around plant roots.
All Crops
🌊 Jeevamrit Bio-Fertilizer
Problem: Chemical-damaged soil.
Recipe: 10kg dung + 10L urine + 2kg jaggery + 2kg besan in 200L water.
How to use: Ferment 7 days. Water 200L per acre.
Natural Zero-Budget Farming
🍂 Dry Leaf Mulch
Problem: Soil drying too fast.
Recipe: 3-inch layer of fallen dry leaves.
How to use: Spread over soil to keep roots cool and wet.
Year-Round Soil Care
🌳 Dhaincha Green Manure
Problem: Low nitrogen in soil.
Recipe: Sow Dhaincha seeds; plough into soil after 45 days.
How to use: Adds natural organic nitrogen.
Wheat & Paddy Fields
🫙 Steamed Bone Meal
Problem: Low phosphorus in soil.
Recipe: Natural bone powder mixed with compost.
How to use: Mix 50 kg per acre before sowing.
Root Vegetables & Pulses
🪨 Rock Phosphate
Problem: Acidic hill soil.
Recipe: Natural mineral rock powder.
How to use: Gives slow-release phosphorus for 3 years.
Hill Agriculture
🌊 Seaweed Extract
Problem: Slow plant growth.
Recipe: 2ml natural seaweed liquid in 1L water.
How to use: Spray on leaves to boost growth by 15–20%.
All Commercial Crops

Recipe: Soak 5 banana peels in water for 10 days.
How to use: Ultra-rich in natural potassium.
Tomato, Chilli, Fruit Plants
