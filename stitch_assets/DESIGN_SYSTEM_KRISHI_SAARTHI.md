## Aesthetic Philosophy: Tactile Pragmatism & Sovereign Agricultural Authority
Engineered for Indian agrarian ecosystems. Operating at the intersection of GovTech authority and edge-computed AgriTech, the aesthetic rejects superficial techno-minimalism in favor of "Tactile Pragmatism"—an ultra-legible, sunlight-resilient visual architecture built for high glare, varying literacy levels, and intermittent connectivity.

### Personality & Values
- **Sovereign & Grounded:** Rooted in the visual dignity of Indian agricultural cycles, leveraging deep organic foliage greens and golden harvest tones with governmental precision.
- **Resilient & Transparent:** The interface never conceals operational state. Offline caching, peer-to-peer data transport, and local edge-inference states are treated as primary ambient indicators.
- **Glanceable & High-Decibel:** Prioritizes heavy visual hierarchy, fortified touch targets (minimum 48dp), and robust contrast ratios (exceeding WCAG AAA standards for critical field data) to survive direct sunlight on lower-tier IPS smartphone displays.

- Headline Large: Plus Jakarta Sans, 28px/24px, 700 weight
- Headline Medium: Plus Jakarta Sans, 20px, 700 weight
- Headline Small: Plus Jakarta Sans, 16px, 700 weight
- Body Medium: Noto Sans, 14px, 400 weight (Line height: 20px)

- Data / Telemetry Mono: JetBrains Mono, 12px, 500 weight
